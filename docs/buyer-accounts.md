# Buyers' accounts

Buyers find their orders at *My account > Orders*, with what is
paid, what is due and how to pay it, and their lease payments.

## Getting an account

Buyers don't sign up first. They check out as guests, and checkout makes their
account and emails them how to sign in, so they can come back to their order. Where
[leases](lease-to-own.md) are on, visitors can also sign up, and go straight on with
checkout, as a lease needs an account.

## What they see

*My account > Orders* lists their orders. Each order shows its number, when it was
placed, its state, the domain, the total, what is paid and what is due. With a
balance due by bank transfer, it repeats your payment instructions, and with a lease,
the payments made and to come.

Buyers aren't asked for two-factor authentication: only the Owner and the Assistant
are. See [Sign-in protection](sign-in-protection.md).

Offers don't need an account: the buyer answers from the link in their email.
