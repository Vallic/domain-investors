# Point parked domains at the lander

At each registrar, forward your parked domains to this site:

- **To the domain's own page**, such as `https://YOUR-SITE/domain/example.com`
  (the address depends on your URL aliases). This is the surest.
- **To the lander with the domain named**:
  `https://YOUR-SITE/domain-lander?domain=example.com`.
- **To the lander alone**, `https://YOUR-SITE/domain-lander`, as one address for
  many domains. The lander then goes by the Referer, which browsers often leave
  out.

The lander sends visitors to the domain's page here. Visitors it can't match,
and domains not for sale, go to the front page.

The dashboard's "Point parked domains at this site" step is optional, as
forwarding straight to a domain's page can't be seen from the site. It shows as
done once a visitor comes through the lander.
